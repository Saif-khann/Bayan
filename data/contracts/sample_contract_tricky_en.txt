EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This contract is made between Al-Noor Trading Co., Riyadh (the "Employer") and
Faisal Al-Otaibi, a Saudi national (the "Employee").

1. Term: This is an indefinite-term contract.

2. Probation: The Employee shall be subject to a probation period of 180 days.

3. Annual leave: The Employee is entitled to 30 days of paid annual leave per year.

4. Notice: To terminate this contract, the Employer must give 60 days' written notice and the Employee must give 30 days' written notice.

5. Non-compete: For two years after this contract ends, the Employee shall not work for a direct competitor in building-materials trading within Riyadh.

6. Probation termination: During the probation period, only the Employer may terminate this contract.

7. Overtime: Overtime hours shall be paid at the hourly rate of the basic wage plus 50%.

8. Loans: The Employer may deduct up to 10% of the monthly wage to recover any loan it has given the Employee.

9. Resignation: A resignation shall take effect on a future date chosen by the Employee in the resignation letter, up to three months later.

10. Marriage leave: The Employee is entitled to 3 days of paid leave for his marriage.

11. Wages: Wages shall be paid in cash at the end of each quarter.
