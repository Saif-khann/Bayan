EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This contract is made between Al-Noor Trading Co., Riyadh (the "Employer") and
Ahmed Khan, an Indian national holding a valid residence permit (the "Employee").

1. Position: The Employee is employed as Sales Coordinator at the Employer's Riyadh office, starting 1 November 2026.

2. Term: This is an indefinite-term contract with no fixed end date.

3. Salary: The Employee shall receive a monthly basic salary of SAR 6,000, paid by bank transfer before the 5th day of each month.

4. Probation: The Employee shall be subject to a probation period of 200 days.

5. Working hours: The Employee shall work 8 hours per day, five days per week, with Friday and Saturday as weekly rest days.

6. Annual leave: The Employee is entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per year.

7. Sick leave: Sick leave shall be granted in accordance with the Labor Law.

8. Termination: Either party may terminate this contract by giving the other party 15 days of written notice.

9. End of service: The Employee waives any right to an end-of-service award.

10. Government fees: The Employee shall pay all fees for his residence permit (Iqama) and work permit, including renewals.

11. Damages: If the Employee damages company property, the Employer may deduct the full cost of repair from his salary in a single month.

12. Non-compete: For three years after this contract ends, the Employee shall not work for any competitor anywhere in the Kingdom.

13. Confidentiality: The Employee shall keep the Employer's trade secrets confidential during and after employment.

14. Governing law: This contract is governed by the Labor Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and disputes shall be referred to the competent labor court.
